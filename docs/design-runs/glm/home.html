About

I am a medical-equipment engineer at the Mogilev Regional Oncology Dispensary, where I maintain Varian TrueBeam / VitalBeam linear accelerators. That work is safety-critical and documentation-heavy — which is exactly why I built Engineer Companion: a field engineer in an RF-shielded bunker needs the exact page in seconds, with no internet.

I have been building software alongside that role since 2020: a commercial geodetic converter, a production photo-to-DXF pipeline, an offline RAG system, and a live Telegram product. I am leaving the clinical role in November 2026 to work on software full-time.

Commercial projects are covered by NDA. I can show architecture, screenshots, and measured results; source stays private.
