Engineer Companion

A field engineer in an RF-shielded bunker needs the exact page of a specification in seconds, with no internet — out of tens of thousands of pages of manuals.

Problem

A field engineer servicing a clinical linear accelerator works in an RF-shielded bunker with no internet and needs the exact page of a specification in seconds — out of tens of thousands of pages of manuals.

Approach

  1. One Python core: PDF indexing, chunking, embeddings, LanceDB retrieval and a fact catalogue.

  2. Hybrid retrieval: dense vectors + BM25, with a cross-encoder reranker.

  3. Local quantized inference (Gemma-3-4B, GGUF / llama.cpp); every answer carries an evidence link to document, page and drawing region.
